## Motivation

When learning a new piece on the guitar, I often found the most time-consuming part to be finding the notes in the staff on the guitar fretboard.
Additionally, the guitar allows you to play the same note on different strings, so there is usually not one single way of playing a combination of notes.
Guitar Assistant shows you all possibilities, so you can pick the one that works for you.

## Usage

1. Head over to https://stockifab.github.io/GuitarAssistant/
2. Pick the notes you need in the staff to the left of your screen; dots on the fretboard show you where to press down, highlighted strings indicate you can play an open string.
You can choose between flats/sharps left to the name of the note in the staff.
3. Need a note higher/lower than what's shown in the staff? Resize the fretboard using the buttons with the appropriate arrow.
